Common questions

Where are my scans stored? Only on your iPhone, inside Scan.me. Use Settings → Back up library to save a copy to iCloud Drive or Files.

I deleted a document by mistake. Open the ••• menu on the home page → Recently deleted. Documents stay there for 30 days.

I forgot my Scan.me passcode. On the lock screen tap Forgot passcode? and confirm with Face ID or your iPhone passcode, then choose a new one.

Scans look blurry or the page edges are wrong. Keep the whole page in view with a little of the table around it, in good light, and hold still until the scanner captures.

Can it read Arabic? Yes, on iPhones whose iOS supports Arabic text recognition. Settings shows whether yours does.
