Scan.me Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Scan.me is a document scanner for iPhone, published by Mohamed Elgebaly (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

  • Your scans stay on your phone. We do not upload them anywhere.
  • Text recognition runs on the phone itself.
  • We do not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • A file only leaves the app when you choose to share it.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you create in Scan.me is stored inside the app on your phone:

  • the page images you scan or import, and your edits to them (corners, rotation, filters, brightness and contrast);
  • the text read from your pages, which is used for search, for suggested document names and for the Receipts list (merchant, date, total);
  • document titles, folders and tags;
  • your settings, such as appearance, language and scanning options.

We cannot see any of this. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

If you use your iPhone’s own iCloud Backup or a computer backup, Apple’s backup of your phone may include the app’s data, as it does for most apps. That backup is handled by Apple under your Apple account and Apple’s privacy policy, not by us.

Deleting a document in Scan.me removes it from the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the phone.

Text recognition

Scan.me reads the text on your pages so you can search, copy and export it. On iPhone this uses Apple’s Vision framework, which is part of iOS and runs on the device. The pages are not sent to us or to any online service for this.

Permissions

Scan.me asks for these permissions, and uses them only for the purpose shown:

Permission Why What happens to the data
Camera To scan documents, ID cards, books and QR codes Photos are turned into pages and kept in the app on your phone
Photos To import photos you pick as scanned pages Only the photos you choose are read; they stay on your phone

You can turn either permission off at any time in the iPhone’s Settings app. The rest of the app keeps working; only scanning with the camera or importing from Photos needs them.

When you open a link, phone number, email address or other content from a QR code, the app hands it to the matching app on your phone (for example Safari, Phone or Mail), and only when you tap to do so. What happens next is covered by that app’s privacy policy.

Sharing, exports and backups

Files leave Scan.me only when you share them yourself, using the iPhone share sheet: for example a PDF, images, a Word or Excel file, text, or a backup file. You choose where they go (Mail, Messages, Files, iCloud Drive or another app), and that service’s own privacy policy then applies.

  • Password-protected PDFs: when you set a password on an exported PDF, the file is encrypted on the phone. We never see or keep the password.
  • Backups: “Back up library” in Settings creates one .scanme file containing all your documents, their text, tags and folders. It is made only when you ask for it, and you choose where to save it. The backup file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere you trust. “Restore from backup” reads a backup file you pick and adds its documents to the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or sell personal data.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking services, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not require an account or sign-in.
  • We do not access your contacts, location, microphone or health data.

Children

Scan.me is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live).

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data, we hold nothing about you that we could show, correct, export or delete. Your scans are under your control in the app: you can share, export or delete them at any time. If you have a question about your privacy rights under the law where you live (for example the GDPR in the EU and UK), contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Scan.me changes how data is handled (for example by adding an optional online feature), we will update this policy before that version is released, change the date at the top, and describe the change in the app’s release notes. Any feature that sends data off your phone will be optional and will ask you first.
